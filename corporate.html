Can you work with our event planner and venue?

Yes. We coordinate with planners, venues and internal teams on entertainment timing, sound checks and load-in.

Can the music reflect our company culture?

Yes. Your audience, musical preferences and event style inform the entertainment plan.

Can we include live musicians?

Yes. Hybrid and live music options are available, subject to performer availability and your event requirements.

Can you provide audio for speeches and awards?

Yes. Share your program and venue details so the appropriate audio coverage can be defined in your proposal.

What do you need to prepare a proposal?

Your date, location, event type, approximate guest count, schedule and entertainment budget provide a useful starting point.
